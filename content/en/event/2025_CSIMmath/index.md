abstract: To promote the exchange of the latest research findings among experts and scholars in mathematical life sciences from around the world, the 4th Mathematical Life Sciences Conference will be held at Renmin University of China (Tongzhou Campus) from August 8 to 10, 2025. The conference is hosted by the Chinese Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics (CSIAM), co-organized by the CSIAM Mathematical Life Sciences Committee, the Institute of Interdisciplinary Sciences, and the School of Mathematics at Renmin University of China, and co-sponsored by the National Governance Big Data and Artificial Intelligence Innovation Platform at Renmin University of China. The conference will focus on cutting-edge areas such as computational systems biology, bioinformatics, artificial intelligence and life sciences, ecology and epidemiology, and Big Data for a Healthy China, systematically showcasing the latest research advances and innovative achievements in these fields. Furthermore, to foster the growth of young scholars and promote cross-disciplinary dialogue, the conference will feature special sessions on "Outstanding Doctoral Student Achievements" and "Healthy China and Precision Medicine," aiming to provide a high-level platform for academic exchange and jointly promote innovation and development in mathematical life sciences research.
