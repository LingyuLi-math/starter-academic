The [HKU Overseas Fellowship Awards](https://www.rss.hku.hk/funding/hku-overseas-fellowship) are an internationalisation programme funded by the University Research Committee (URC) and administered by Research Services. Together with related URC schemes such as the King’s/HKU Fellowship Awards and the Glasgow/HKU Early Career Mobility Funds, the awards support HKU researchers to visit overseas institutions for research collaboration and academic exchange.

I received this award (HK$50,000) to support an overseas research visit.
