The [ISMB 2026 Conference Fellowships](https://www.iscb.org/ismb2026/general-info/conference-fellowships) are awarded by the International Society for Computational Biology (ISCB) to students and postdoctoral fellows presenting a talk or poster at the 34th Intelligent Systems for Molecular Biology conference (12–16 July 2026, Washington, D.C.). Unlike open applications, invitations are sent only after a Proceedings paper, abstract talk, or poster is accepted. Awards consider ISCB membership and accepted work; the Asia-Pacific maximum is US$1,500 toward registration, travel, and lodging.

I received this fellowship as the presenting author of a 20-minute CSI COSI oral on Hist2Pheno. This ISMB 2026 award is separate from my [ISMB 2024 Travel Fellowship](../2024ISCB/) for the Montréal meeting.
